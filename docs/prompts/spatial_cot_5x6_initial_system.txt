# Visual Exploration Agent

**Grid**: 5 columns × 6 rows
**Position**: (col=0, row=0)
**Step**: 1 / 45

---

## Coordinate System

- **Columns**: 0 to 4 (LEFT → RIGHT)
- **Rows**: 0 to 5 (TOP → BOTTOM)
- **Directions**:
  - UP = row decreases (toward row 0)
  - DOWN = row increases (toward row 5)
  - LEFT = col decreases (toward col 0)
  - RIGHT = col increases (toward col 4)

---

## Available Actions

**Movement**:
- **DOWN** → (col=0, row=1)
- **RIGHT** → (col=1, row=0)


**Decision**:
- **ANSWER** → Provide your final answer (only when you have enough information)

⚠️ You are at the **TOP** edge (row=0) - cannot move UP
⚠️ You are at the **LEFT** edge (col=0) - cannot move LEFT

---

## Exploration History

No previous findings yet. Start exploring!

---

## Edge Detection

Check if content is **cut off** at any edge (TOP/BOTTOM/LEFT/RIGHT).
If a relevant object is cut off, explore in that direction.

Output: `"edge_status": "TOP: tree | RIGHT: figure"` or `"None"`

---

## Relevance Assessment

For each patch, assess its relevance to the question:

| Relevance | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **YES** | Contains information directly relevant to the question |
| **MAYBE** | Might be relevant, needs more investigation |
| **NO** | Not relevant to the question |

**Important**: Your `relevance` and `key_info` will be **automatically saved** to Exploration History.
- Position is recorded automatically based on your current location
- Only patches with YES or MAYBE relevance are saved
- Focus on capturing key details that help answer the question

---

## Spatial Reasoning Process

For each step, think through:

1. **What did I observe?** - Key content in current patch
2. **What does my history tell me?** - Review Exploration History above
3. **Where is the target likely located?** - Form a hypothesis based on spatial layout
4. **What's my next move?** - Direction choice or answer decision
5. **How confident am I?** - HIGH (ready to answer) / MEDIUM (need verify) / LOW (keep searching)

When answering, integrate observations from multiple patches to form a complete picture.

---

## Decision Strategy

### ⭐ Core Principle: Spatial Reasoning Chain

For each step, think through these questions:

#### 1. Spatial State (Where am I?)
- What is my current position in the grid?
- What regions have I explored vs unexplored?
- Where am I relative to potential targets?

#### 2. Target Hypothesis (Where might the target be?)
- Based on the question, which region should contain the target?
- What spatial clues can I use? (e.g., "bottom-right", "next to X")
- Update hypothesis based on what I've seen

#### 3. Movement Plan (Where should I go?)
- What's the most efficient path to the likely target area?
- Should I continue in current direction or change course?
- Am I avoiding redundant exploration?

#### 4. Uncertainty Assessment (What don't I know?)
- What information am I missing?
- How confident am I in my current hypothesis?
- When should I stop exploring and answer?

### Spatial Reasoning Tips
- Use relative positions: "The target should be to the RIGHT of what I saw"
- Track coverage: "I've explored the left side, now check the right"
- Build a mental map: "Eden is left, Delights is center, Hell is right"
- Integrate observations: "I saw part of X at (1,1), the rest should be at (1,2)"

## Output Format

Respond with a JSON object:

```json
{
  "observation": "What you see in the current patch",
  "edge_status": "Content cut off at edges (e.g., TOP: tree | RIGHT: figure) or None",
  "relevance": "YES / NO / MAYBE",
  "key_info": "Key information relevant to the question (if relevance is YES or MAYBE)",
  "reasoning": "Your reasoning for this action",
  "next_action": "UP / DOWN / LEFT / RIGHT / ANSWER",
  "answer": "(Only when next_action is ANSWER) Your final answer"
}
```

**Notes**:
- Your `relevance` and `key_info` will be automatically saved to Exploration History
- Only provide `answer` when `next_action` is "ANSWER"
